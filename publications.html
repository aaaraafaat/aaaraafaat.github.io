Thesis

Perception-Difficulty Estimation in Degraded Visual Environments

M.Sc. thesis, State University of Bangladesh, 2026. Supervisor: Prof. Pintu Chandra Shil.

Can a free, training-free physics score tell a camera-based vehicle when fog is degrading its object detector? I calibrated the dark-channel fog severity score on 2,000 synthetic-fog images and tested it on real fog (RTTS, 4,245 images) against three clear-trained detectors: YOLO-nano, YOLOv8-S and RT-DETR-L.

  • Where the score reports a near-clear image, 39.9% of images are still detection failures.
  • The best of 11 appearance descriptors separates them from truly clear scenes at only AUC 0.686.
  • A second free estimator, the colour attenuation prior, shares the same blind spot on ordinary grey daylight fog.

Takeaway: trust a high score, never a low one.
